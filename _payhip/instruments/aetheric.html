Keep It Simple

Aetheric is a simple, yet great-sounding ambient pad library full of lush synth pads and natural recorded ambiences designed to spark creativity and set a mood within seconds.

It contains 10 generators, each split into 3 oscillators with individual volume controls to sculpt the sound as you see fit.
