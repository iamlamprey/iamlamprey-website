Overview

Muzzle is a lightweight de-esser for taming harsh sibilance and whistling vocals. I needed something that was fast and flexible, and wanted to integrate part of my daily-driver vocal chain (top-end filters) into a single plugin.

Features

  • Clean split-band de-essing
  • Solo band toggle to target problematic whistles
  • Attack / release / ratio controls
  • Factory presets for different voice types
  • Custom pre-chain filters (PRO)

For Transparency

Muzzle's de-esser core is adapted from two free, MIT-licensed Airwindows plugins by Chris Johnson — DeBess and DeEss. This core is extended with Muzzle's tunable band-pass detection, timing controls and pre-chain filters.

You can view the original DSP at github.com/airwindows.
