Features

Two Amplifiers

Altar comes with a glassy clean channel, and a modern high-gain channel for a wide variety of tones.

NAM Support

Load your existing NAM (Neural Amp Modeler) files by switching to Altar's third amp channel. Supports both A1 and A2 model formats.

Modular DSP

Drag the amp, cab and FX modules around to re-order them. Right click to bypass.

Cab Designer

Create your own professional-sounding IRs with an intuitive designer tool. Share your cabs with friends or even sell them.

Pitch Shifting

Two-octave realtime pitch shifting with semitone-snapping and a sub-octave layer blend, all fully automatable.

FX Suite

Multiple Overdrive modes, Circuit-Bending, Reverb, Forward, Reverse and Glitch Delays, Chorus and a Ringmod.

Mixing Tools

Mix faster with the Low-frequency palm mute control (Chug). Upper-mid pick attack expansion (Pick), a pre-set Whistle filter and built-in Lofi effect.

Pre & Post Process

Process the DI and final tone with industry-grade channel strips for the ultimate encapsulated tone.

No Stone Unturned

Up to 8x Oversampling, built-in Tuner, Click, and an Output Limiter complete the package for your new amplifier.
